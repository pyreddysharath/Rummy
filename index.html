    How it works
    Scoring

    Add players with ✎ in the Player heading, type each round's points and tap Save. A player is OUT on reaching the total limit. The i next to Scoreboard explains every colour and icon.

    Playing online with friends

    Open Play Online, give the room a name and tap Create Room. Share the Edit link with players who enter scores and the View link with people who only watch – on WhatsApp or anywhere. Scores update on every phone straight away, no refresh needed. Nobody needs to log in.

    Keeping your rooms

    As a guest, your rooms are listed on this device. Keep each room's Owner link (WhatsApp it to yourself) – it brings back full control after clearing the browser or on a new phone. Or sign in with Google once, and your rooms follow your account on every device.

    When rooms are deleted

    Games are deleted after a period with no activity – up to 90 days for guests, up to 1 year when signed in – and a room once it has no games left. The owner chooses the period in Room Settings. Use Export Data to keep a copy for longer.

    Calculating shares

    Set the Entry or buy-in per player in Game Settings, then tap ₹ Calculate Shares under the scoreboard. Pick how to split the pot among the players still in – the i next to each option explains it – and tap Share to send the result.

    Backups

    Export Data saves a file with the games on this device and any rooms you tick. Import Data brings them back – rooms come back with new links.

    Feedback
    Your feedback has been fed back. Thanks!

    On this device: players, scores, settings and previous games are saved in your browser's local storage. They are not sent anywhere unless you use an online room.

    Online rooms: when you create or open a room, its name, player names, scores and game times are stored in Google Firebase so that everyone with the room's link can see them. You are signed in with an anonymous ID; if you choose Sign in with Google, your Google account's name and email are used only to keep your rooms on your account. Anyone who has a room's link can see that room – share links only with people you trust. Rooms and games are deleted automatically after the period without activity shown in Room Settings, or when the owner deletes them.

    Feedback: if you send feedback, the name and message you enter are sent to us for review. Please don't include personal or sensitive details.

    No ads or tracking: this site shows no advertising and uses no analytics or advertising cookies.

    Rummy Scoreboard is a free score-keeping tool for friendly games. It doesn't take, hold or pay any money: the share calculator only does the arithmetic for a split your group has agreed on. Playing for money is restricted in some states (for example Telangana) – follow the law where you play. Check the totals yourself – the app is provided as it is, without any guarantee, and we're not responsible for disputes or for scores lost through cleared browsers, expired rooms or connection problems.
