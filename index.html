    How it works
    1. Start a game

    Tap New Game. The first time, give the room a name – the occasion, e.g. "Diwali at Ravi's" – then check the game name, players and Game Rules and tap Create & Start. For the next game, tap New Game again: the room is already chosen, or pick another one or ＋ Create a new room.

    2. Enter scores

    Type each round's points and tap Save. Add someone later with ＋ Add player under the list. A player is OUT on reaching the total limit. The i next to Scoreboard explains every colour and icon.

    3. Play together on several phones

    Share the room's Edit link with players who enter scores and the View link with people who only watch. Scores update on every phone straight away. Nobody needs to log in.

    4. Calculate shares

    Set the Entry or buy-in per player in the Game Rules (New Game, or Game details → Edit rules), then tap ₹ Calculate Shares under the scoreboard and pick how to split the pot – the i next to each option explains it.

    5. My Rooms

    Tap My Rooms to see every room – newest first – with its games. Tap ＋ to open a room, a game to see its scoreboard, the link icon for the View and Edit links, and the bin to delete a room or a game (you have 5 seconds to Undo). Deleting a room's last game deletes the room too.

    6. Keep your rooms

    As a guest, your rooms are listed on this device only. Sign in with Google from the menu (top right) and your rooms follow you to every device.

    7. When games are deleted

    The same for everyone: a game is deleted automatically after 90 days with no play, and a room once it has no games left.

    8. Backups

    Export data (in the menu) saves a file with your rooms and games. Import Data brings them back – rooms come back with new links.

    9. Install as an app

    Tap Install App in the menu (top right) or the install icon in the browser's address bar. On iPhone or iPad (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen.
